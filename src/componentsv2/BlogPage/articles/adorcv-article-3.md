# AI Matching in AdorCV: Connecting Job Descriptions with Existing CVs

## How AdorCV's dedicated Job Descriptions section uses AI to match required skills to candidates

Beyond CV standardization and database management, AdorCV includes a dedicated section for Job Descriptions that enables AI matching of required skills between job descriptions and existing CVs. This article focuses on this specific feature and how it fits into AdorCV's broader goal of helping HR teams save time and improve efficiency across recruitment workflows.

### What the Job Descriptions Section Does

Within AdorCV, users have access to a dedicated section for Job Descriptions. This section is built specifically to compare the requirements of a role against the candidate CVs already stored in the AdorCV database. Rather than treating job descriptions and candidate CVs as separate, disconnected documents, AdorCV brings them together within the same application.

### How AI Matching Works

AdorCV's AI matching identifies the required skills listed in a job description and compares them against the skills present in existing CVs within the database. This allows HR teams to see which candidates already in their database align with the requirements of a given role, without needing to manually review each CV individually.

Because this matching is based on required skills, it gives HR teams a structured starting point when evaluating which candidates might be relevant to a new or existing job description.

### Why This Matters for HR Teams

Matching job requirements to candidate skills is a core part of recruitment, and doing it manually across a CV database takes time. By automating this comparison, AdorCV's Job Descriptions section supports one of the application's central goals: helping HR teams save time and improve efficiency across recruitment workflows.

This is especially relevant as a candidate database grows. The more CVs an HR team has stored, the more time it would normally take to manually compare each one against a new job description. AI matching removes this manual step, allowing HR teams to focus their time on reviewing the candidates that have already been identified as relevant.

### How AI Matching Connects to Other AdorCV Features

#### Built on Standardized CV Data

AI matching works with the CVs stored in AdorCV's database — the same CVs that were automatically converted into a unified, company-branded structure when uploaded. Because this data follows a consistent structure, it can be compared against job description requirements more effectively than a mix of differently formatted documents.

#### Supported by the Searchable CV Database

AdorCV's searchable CV database with advanced filters works alongside the Job Descriptions section, giving HR teams more than one way to identify relevant candidates — either through AI matching against a specific job description or through direct filtering and search.

#### Editable Profiles Keep Matching Relevant

Since AdorCV includes tools to edit or enrich candidate profiles, the information used in AI matching can be kept up to date, helping ensure that skill comparisons reflect a candidate's current profile rather than outdated information.

#### Controlled Access Through Permissions

As with other parts of AdorCV, access to the Job Descriptions section and the matching results it produces is governed by user-based login and role-specific permissions, so that only appropriate team members can view or act on this information.

#### Available in English and French

As with the rest of AdorCV, the Job Descriptions section supports documents in both English and French, in line with the application's overall multilingual support.

### Benefits of AI-Powered Job and CV Matching

- **Faster identification of relevant candidates**, without manually reviewing every CV in the database
- **Better use of an existing CV database**, by systematically comparing stored candidate skills against new job requirements
- **Consistent matching criteria**, based on the required skills defined in each job description
- **Time savings for HR teams**, supporting more efficient recruitment workflows overall
- **A structured starting point for evaluation**, since matched candidates can then be reviewed further using the searchable database and advanced filters
