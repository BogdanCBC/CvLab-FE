# AdorCV: The AI Application That Automates HR and Recruitment Tasks

## An overview of how AdorCV helps HR teams standardize, manage, and match candidate CVs

HR and recruitment teams handle a large amount of administrative work every day. Candidate CVs arrive in different formats, need to be reviewed, organized, stored, and eventually matched against open roles. Each of these steps takes time, and when done manually across many candidates and many open positions, that time adds up quickly. AdorCV is an AI application built specifically to automate and streamline these key HR and recruitment tasks within teams, reducing manual work and improving consistency across the entire hiring process.

This article gives an overview of what AdorCV does, the features it includes, and the benefits it offers to HR teams looking to bring more structure and efficiency to how they manage candidate data.

### Why HR Teams Need This Kind of Automation

Recruitment involves a large volume of repetitive administrative work: reviewing CVs, organizing them, keeping profiles up to date, and comparing candidates against role requirements. When this work is done manually, it takes time away from the parts of recruitment that require human judgment, such as evaluating fit and communicating with candidates. AdorCV is built to automate and streamline exactly these key HR and recruitment tasks, so that formatting, organizing, and searching candidate data no longer require constant manual effort.

### What AdorCV Does

AdorCV allows users to upload candidate CVs in various formats and automatically converts them into a unified, company-branded structure. This ensures consistency across all documents, regardless of how each CV originally arrived — whether as a Word document, a PDF, or another common file type.

Once a CV has been processed, it can be exported in the format that best fits the situation: PDF, editable DOCX, or PPT. This gives HR teams flexibility depending on whether a document needs to be shared internally, sent externally, or presented.

### Key Features of AdorCV

#### Automated CV Standardization

Candidate CVs uploaded in various formats are automatically converted into a unified, company-branded structure. This removes the need to manually reformat each document and ensures every candidate profile looks consistent, no matter how differently each candidate originally submitted their CV.

#### Flexible Export Options

Once standardized, CVs can be exported as PDF, editable DOCX, or PPT files, depending on the format required for a given step in the recruitment process. This means the same candidate profile can be adapted to different needs without additional manual formatting.

#### User-Based Login and Role-Specific Permissions

AdorCV includes user-based login with role-specific permissions, allowing organizations to control who can access, edit, or manage candidate data within the platform. Each user works from their own account, and their level of access can be set according to their role.

#### Searchable CV Database with Advanced Filters

All candidate CVs are stored in a searchable database with advanced filters, making it easier to locate specific profiles based on relevant criteria rather than manually browsing through every stored CV.

#### Tools to Edit or Enrich Candidate Profiles

Candidate profiles are not static once uploaded. AdorCV includes tools that allow HR teams to edit or enrich profiles, keeping candidate information accurate and up to date as circumstances change.

#### AI Matching Between Job Descriptions and CVs

A dedicated section for Job Descriptions enables AI matching of required skills between job descriptions and existing CVs in the database. This helps HR teams identify relevant candidates for a role based on the skills already documented in their CV database, without reviewing every profile manually.

#### Fully Customizable Templates

Templates used for standardized CVs are fully customizable, allowing companies to align exported documents with their own branding and formatting preferences rather than relying on a fixed, generic layout.

#### Multilingual Support

AdorCV supports documents in both English and French, allowing HR teams to generate and manage candidate profiles in either language within the same system.

### How These Features Work Together

Each feature in AdorCV supports the others. Standardized CVs feed into a consistent, searchable database. That database, in turn, is what makes AI matching against job descriptions possible, since the AI is comparing structured, consistent candidate data rather than a mix of unrelated formats. Role-specific permissions ensure that as more people within an HR team use the database, access remains controlled and appropriate. And because templates are customizable and available in English and French, this entire workflow can be adapted to a company's branding and language requirements.

### Benefits for HR Teams

By combining these features, AdorCV helps HR teams save time and improve efficiency across recruitment workflows.

- **Less manual formatting work**, since CVs are automatically standardized into a unified structure
- **Easier collaboration**, supported by role-specific permissions and a centralized database
- **Faster access to relevant candidates**, through advanced search filters and AI-powered matching
- **Consistent, branded documentation**, thanks to fully customizable templates
- **Support for bilingual operations**, with documents available in English and French

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What file formats can AdorCV export CVs to?

AdorCV can export standardized candidate profiles as PDF, editable DOCX, or PPT files.

#### Does AdorCV support languages other than English?

Yes, AdorCV supports documents in both English and French.

AdorCV includes a dedicated Job Descriptions section where AI matches the required skills listed in a job description against the skills found in existing CVs in the database.

#### Can candidate data access be restricted within AdorCV?

Yes, AdorCV features user-based login with role-specific permissions, allowing control over who can access or manage candidate data.

Yes, AdorCV includes tools to edit or enrich candidate profiles at any time after they have been added to the database.

### Who AdorCV Is Built For

AdorCV is designed for HR professionals and recruiters who regularly work with candidate CVs — reviewing new applications, maintaining a database of past candidates, and comparing candidates against open roles. Because the application automates and streamlines HR and recruitment tasks within teams, it is built to be used collaboratively, with multiple team members accessing the same standardized database under their own permissions.

### Final Thoughts

AdorCV brings together CV standardization, secure access control, a searchable candidate database, AI-powered job matching, and multilingual support in a single application. For HR teams looking to reduce manual administrative work and bring more consistency to their recruitment process, AdorCV offers a practical, AI-driven way to manage candidate CVs from upload to export.
